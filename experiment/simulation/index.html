      What to Observe

      As you move the frequency near f₁, the ring's coupled field amplitude rises and the standing-wave envelope becomes more pronounced — the resonator is absorbing more energy from the feed line. Away from resonance the ring stays faint and the envelope flattens toward a circle.

      Watch how energy must cross the coupling gap between each feed line and the ring — this gap is what couples power in and out of the resonator.
